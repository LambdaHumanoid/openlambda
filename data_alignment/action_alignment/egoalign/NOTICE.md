# Attribution and third-party notices

EgoAlign provides bilateral endpoint optimization and integrates SONIC utilities
from NVIDIA's GR00T-WholeBodyControl. Runtime source and patch identities are
recorded in `runtime/patches/manifest.json`.

The pinned runtime repository contains its upstream source-code and model-weight
notices; consult that repository when obtaining the runtime. Model weights and
SMPL/G1 assets are obtained separately. Inherited transforms retain their source
references and attribution; third-party components retain their original terms.

See [docs/THIRD_PARTY.md](docs/THIRD_PARTY.md) for component provenance and attribution.
